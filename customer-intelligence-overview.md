# Customer Intelligence Overview

## Why This Exists

When I joined TEQ Connect as the founding CS hire, there was no system for tracking customer health, no segmented engagement strategy, and no shared knowledge about what was working and what wasn't. The information existed — in conversations, in calls, in my head — but it wasn't documented, structured, or usable by anyone else.

I built this system because I knew from my startup experience that understanding your customers strategically is what separates companies that retain and grow accounts from companies that lose them. I brought it up in a 1:1 with our CTO, started building it, and when I showed him the finished product, his response was: "This is a lot more than I expected."

## How It Works

The system has three connected layers:

**Customer Profiles** capture what's happening at the individual account level — the real situation, the risks, the active strategy, and what I've learned from each account. These are living documents that evolve as the account evolves.

**Customer Playbooks** define how we engage specific types of customers based on observed patterns. We have three segments: Pilot (evaluating, time-limited, high risk of stalling), Enterprise (multi-year, complex, high-touch), and Complex Multi-Year (milestone-driven, customization-heavy). Each playbook was built from real account experiences, not templates.

**Cross-Customer Insights** aggregate what I'm seeing across all accounts into organizational knowledge — recurring pain points, adoption blockers, engagement drivers, and expansion signals. This is where individual account lessons become team-wide strategy.

## What We're Seeing

The patterns that have emerged across accounts:

- Pilot customers stall when their initial position goes on hold, and if you're not proactively reaching out, you lose them
- The talent shortage in trades is a systemic issue that affects platform usage regardless of how good the product is
- Enterprise customers get overwhelmed when you try to configure too many positions at once
- Customers adopt when the platform replaces their phone screen step, not when it's positioned as an add-on
- Early demonstration of value — through real or sample candidates — is the strongest predictor of engagement
- Companies name positions differently, so aligning on tasks and traits matters more than matching a job title

## What We're Doing About It

- Pivoting pilot customers to new positions immediately when the initial one falls through, rather than waiting
- Running sample candidate evaluations to create value even when active hiring is delayed
- Using the milestone approach with enterprise customers — one position at a time, weekly working sessions
- Positioning the platform as a replacement for phone screens in every onboarding conversation
- Providing stakeholder input templates to reduce the coordination burden on internal champions
- Standardizing these approaches through documented playbooks so the strategy is consistent and scalable

## Why This Matters

This system moves TEQ Connect from reactive account management — responding to problems after they happen — to a proactive, pattern-driven model where we anticipate risks, standardize our best responses, and capture what we learn so it benefits every account going forward. With a small customer base where every account matters, that structure is the difference between growth and churn.
