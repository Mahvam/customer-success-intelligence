# Customer Resources

These are the resources I curated and use during customer onboarding, adoption, and ongoing engagement. They support both the employer experience and the candidate experience, because if either side has a bad experience with the platform, adoption suffers.

## Employer Experience

**User Experience Onboarding Slides.** A walkthrough of the platform from the employer's perspective. I use this during initial onboarding to set expectations and build confidence. Customers who understand what they are looking at in the dashboard and the candidate results are more likely to actually use the data in their hiring decisions.

## Candidate Experience

**Employee Experience Assessment.** An overview of what the candidate facing assessment looks like. This matters because hiring managers need to understand what their applicants will experience. One pilot customer was concerned about adding extra steps for candidates. Being able to show them exactly what the candidate sees, and how lightweight it is, answers that objection before it becomes an adoption blocker.

## ATS Integration

**ADP ATS Integration.** A technical guide for connecting the platform with ADP's applicant tracking system. For customers already using ADP, having assessment results flow into their existing system removes a workflow friction point. It also reinforces the positioning that the platform fits into their process rather than sitting alongside it.

## Hiring Setup

**Submitting Your Own Interview Questions.** A guide for customers who want to customize the interview questions used in their assessments. This matters most for enterprise and complex accounts, where safety critical roles need specific scenario based questions. A safety critical customer, for example, needed conscientiousness focused questions across every assessment, and this guide shows them how to submit those.

## Reading Results

**Reading Your Employability Results.** A guide for interpreting candidate assessment results. It helps hiring managers understand scoring, compare candidates, and make informed decisions. The customers who engage deeply with results, reviewing scorecards, discussing them with managers, using them in interview prep, are the ones who stick with the platform.
