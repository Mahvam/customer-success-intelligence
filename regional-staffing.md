# Regional Staffing Group

## Customer Overview

Staffing and recruiting organization evaluating the platform as part of their candidate screening process. As a staffing company, they're placing candidates at client companies — which means the assessment needs to fit into an already-complex workflow where they're managing both employer expectations and candidate flow.

## Customer Type

Pilot — evaluating whether the platform adds enough value to justify integrating it into their existing recruiting process.

## What Happened

We onboarded them and built out a profile for one of their active positions. After several days with no activity, the customer reached out to me and explained that the company they were staffing for had decided to hold off on that position. Same pattern I'd seen before — the initial position falls through and everything stalls.

We're currently working together to identify another position to test with. The key challenge with this account isn't interest — it's finding the right opening to prove value on.

## Key Risks

- The customer has expressed concern about duplicating steps for candidates — they don't want the assessment to feel like extra work on top of their existing screening
- Their existing recruiting workflow is established, and if the platform isn't clearly integrated into it, they'll default to what they've always done
- Inconsistent usage across roles or recruiters means the platform never gets a fair test
- If we don't get them to see results on at least one position, the pilot will expire without conversion

## Current Strategy

Focus on helping the customer define exactly where the platform fits in their recruiting workflow. The positioning matters: this should replace the initial phone screening step, not add to it. If they see it as an extra task, adoption won't stick.

## Next Steps

- Align with the team on a single, clear workflow for when and how to use the assessment
- Identify one role to fully test the process end-to-end
- Encourage sending the assessment to all applicants for that role so we get meaningful data
- Review candidate results together and talk through the impact on their screening time

## What I Learned

Staffing companies are a unique customer type because they're managing a relationship with their client company on top of managing the candidates. When the client company pauses a position, everything downstream stops. The CS strategy for staffing accounts needs to account for that extra layer of dependency — and the pivot has to happen fast because the pilot clock is ticking.

## Expansion Opportunity

If the platform successfully reduces time spent screening candidates and improves candidate quality on one role, there's a path to expanding across additional roles and recruiters within the organization.
