# Customer Resources

These are the resources I've curated and use during customer onboarding, adoption, and ongoing engagement. They support both the employer experience and the candidate experience — because if either side has a bad experience with the platform, adoption suffers.

## Employer Experience

**User Experience Onboarding Slides** — Walkthrough of the platform from the employer's perspective. I use this during initial onboarding to set expectations and build confidence. Customers who understand what they're looking at in the dashboard and the candidate results are more likely to actually use the data in their hiring decisions.

## Candidate Experience

**Employee Experience Assessment** — Overview of what the candidate-facing assessment looks like. This matters because hiring managers need to understand what their applicants will experience. One of our pilot customers expressed concern about adding extra steps for candidates. Being able to show them exactly what the candidate sees — and how lightweight it is — addresses that objection before it becomes an adoption blocker.

## ATS Integration

**ADP/TEQ Connect ATS Integration** — Technical guide for connecting the platform with ADP's applicant tracking system. For customers already using ADP, having assessment results flow into their existing system removes a workflow friction point. It also reinforces the positioning that this platform fits *into* their process rather than sitting alongside it.

## Hiring Setup

**Submitting Your Own Interview Questions** — Guide for customers who want to customize the interview questions used in their assessments. This is particularly important for enterprise and complex customers where safety-critical roles require specific scenario-based questions. Our mining customer, for example, needed conscientiousness-focused questions across every assessment — this guide helps them understand how to submit those.

## Reading Results

**Reading Your Employability Results** — Guide for interpreting candidate assessment results. This helps hiring managers understand scoring, compare candidates, and make informed decisions. The customers who engage deeply with results — reviewing scorecards, discussing them with managers, using them in interview prep — are the ones who stick with the platform.
